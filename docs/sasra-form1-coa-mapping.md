## Calculation and review

Amounts are **KSh**, unlike Form 6 and Form 7's KSh thousands. Balances are cumulative through the selected day across all branches. Journal value date takes precedence over created date. Prior-year unclosed earnings are included in retained earnings. Current-year system closing transfers (transaction code 15) are removed from capital components and income/expense balances so earnings contribute once. Manual closing entries without that code cannot be identified automatically.

The [completion guidance](https://kenyalaw.org/kl/fileadmin/pdfdownloads/LegalNotices/2010/LN95_2010.pdf) includes half of eligible current-year unaudited profit, but the full loss, in core capital. Eligibility requires reviewing provisions, dividends and unrealised items. Investment deductions and off-balance-sheet exposures require supporting schedules. The workbook supplies the 10%, 8% and 8% ratio thresholds.

Enter explicit amounts, including zero where applicable, for surplus eligibility adjustment, subsidiary/equity investment deduction, other deductions and off-balance-sheet exposures. Non-zero inputs require an explanation. These inputs and the review confirmation apply to the preview and are returned in its response; they are **not persisted as a report run or mapping revision**.

The selected Form 6 revision supplies total assets and deposit liabilities for the same reporting dates. Its data issues propagate. Form 1 asset categories must reconcile to Form 6 within KSh 0.01; a separate check verifies the underlying ledger balances. This implementation requires a zero asset reconciliation difference and does not support an attached explanatory reconciliation. Zero/negative ratio denominators produce unavailable ratios and block download. A deficient but correctly calculated ratio produces a warning and remains exportable.

Excel download uses the same generated bytes as preview. The primary amount column D is populated; the original additional Year 3 / Year 4 columns are retained but not populated. Original formulas, labels, formats, row heights, print settings and authorization area are preserved. Original percentage display precision is retained; the UI shows more decimal places. This does not sign or submit a regulatory return.
